# Game Publishing

> A quick guide to publishing your s&box game and making it publicly available on sbox.game.

## Requirements

- Full version of **s&box**
  - Required to publish on **sbox.game**
- **s&box Editor**
  - Required to create, edit, and test the project

## Instructions

1. Create a **Startup Scene**
   - Your game project should define a startup scene in **Project Settings**
   - This is the first scene loaded when the game starts
   - [s&box Docs: Game Project / Startup](https://sbox.game/dev/doc/getting-started/project-types/game-project)
2. Open the **Publish** menu
   - In the top-right corner of the s&box Editor, click your game's name next to the **cog wheel**
   - A dropdown menu will appear
   - Click **Publish**[](https://cdn.sbox.game/upload/b/30a733c5/8372/4ea6/a15f/d28830031725.mp4)
3. Complete the **Publish Wizard**
   - Fill out the required game information
   - Follow each step in the wizard
   - Submit the game once everything is complete
4. Done
   - Your game will now be submitted for publishing on **sbox.game**

## Making Your Game Public

1. Open your **s&box organization**
   - Go to your organization's packages page
   - Example: `https://sbox.game/{orgname}/~packages?type=game`
2. Select your **game**
   - Find the game you just published
   - Click it to open the package page
3. Click **Edit**
   - On the right side of the page, click **Edit**[![](https://cdn.sbox.game/upload/b/e30a73b4/2538/43cc/a640/88f943d63ec4.png)](https://cdn.sbox.game/upload/b/e30a73b4/2538/43cc/a640/88f943d63ec4.png)
4. Fill out the **game information**
   - Add any required information, images, descriptions, or other package details
5. Change the **Publish State**
   - Change the publish state from **Hidden** to **Released**[![](https://cdn.sbox.game/upload/b/b138231d/a3cd/45e7/80b0/4d848cdb576f.png)](https://cdn.sbox.game/upload/b/b138231d/a3cd/45e7/80b0/4d848cdb576f.png)
6. Click **Save Changes**
   - Once everything is filled out correctly, click **Save Changes**
   - Your game will now be publicly visible on **sbox.game**
